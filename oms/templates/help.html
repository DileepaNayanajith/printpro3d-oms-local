PRINTPRO3D · DAILY WORK

A simple handoff.

1. Caller

Call and qualify in Meta. Enter the confirmed recipient, address, exact FDE city, item, quantity, COD and packed weight.

Choose Save confirmed order. It appears at packing immediately. Save a draft if details are missing.

2. Packing

Check item and quantity. Scan/type the assigned CCP sticker, confirm the FDE weight band, then queue the form fill.

Wait for courier confirmation. Print and check the official waybill, attach it to the correct parcel, then mark packed.

3. Courier review

The dedicated FDE browser fills the order. Caller checks every detail before submission.

After submission, find the FDE record and confirm the matching tracking in OMS. Attach the official PDF from FDE Label Print.

If something stops

  • Worker offline: ask the owner to start the FDE browser worker. Leave the order queued.
  • Sign-in needed: sign into the open worker browser, then leave it open.
  • Needs review: check FDE for the waybill/order before retrying. A timeout can happen after a successful booking.
  • Wrong saved details: ask the owner to resolve them; do not use a different waybill to bypass the issue.
  • TEST ONLY: never submit or dispatch sample orders.

Pilot limits

Auto-fill is verified. Automatic submission is off by default; courier success detection, label download and physical printing still need a controlled real-order trial. Do not treat a filled form as a confirmed booking.
