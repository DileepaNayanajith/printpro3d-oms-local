STAFF GUIDE

Four simple steps.

01 · Add your orders

Enter confirmed customer details and use Save order & add next. Add the whole group before printing.

02 · Print labels together

Open Print labels, select the orders and print. Two labels fit on each A4 sheet. Cut down the middle and attach the courier stickers without covering the OMS order barcodes.

03 · Scan and book

Open Scan & book. Scan the OMS order barcode first, then the courier sticker barcode. Use Enter after each scan. The screen clears for the next label; courier bookings process in the background. “Queued” means waiting; “Booked” means FDE confirmed success.

04 · Pack

Use the packing sheet to check items, match the order and courier sticker, attach the label, and mark packed.

If something needs attention

Keep the main computer, print worker and signed-in FDE browser running. A printer queue acknowledgement does not prove paper came out. Check the printer queue before requesting another copy. If an FDE response is uncertain, check its parcel record before retrying.
